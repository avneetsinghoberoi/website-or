What we do

Four ways to build with us

We buy the plot, design the building, construct it and sell each floor with clear title. Small buildings, few homes, one or two per floor.

  • Authorized colonies with documented title
  • Stilt parking, lift and individual water tanks
  • Book early at launch, before construction prices
See current projects

You own the land and keep the whole building. We take it from drawings to handover as a single contractor, on a fixed specification you can check brand by brand.

  • Structure, finishing, electrical and plumbing
  • Turnkey handover, including interiors if you want them
  • Stage-wise payments linked to work done on site
Discuss your plot

You bring an old house or empty plot. We rebuild it at our cost and the new floors are shared between you and us, as agreed in writing before work starts.

  • No construction cost to the plot owner
  • Owner's floors finished to the same standard as ours
  • Share and floor allocation fixed in the agreement
How collaboration works

For homes and buildings you already own. We redo interiors room by room, or give the whole building a new front, with the same team and finish as our own floors.

  • False ceilings, lighting, flooring and wall panelling
  • Modular kitchens, wardrobes and bathrooms
  • Facade elevation, cladding, railings and gates
